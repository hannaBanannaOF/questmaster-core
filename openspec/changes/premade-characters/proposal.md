# Proposal

## Why

A player who receives an invite can only join with a character they already created, of the campaign's game system and not in another campaign. Many tables start with characters the DM prepared (one-shots, new players, convention games), and a player without a sheet has to create one before joining. The `character_sheet` table already allows a null `player_id`, but nothing creates such characters and the domain assumes every character has a player.

## What Changes

- **Premade characters**: the campaign DM creates characters inside the campaign, with no player, through `POST /core/api/v1/campaign/{campaignID}/character` (name and optional max HP; the game system is the campaign's). Only the DM, and not in an ARCHIVED campaign.
- **Choosing one when accepting an invite**: the invite details list the campaign's unclaimed premade characters, and accepting an invite with one of them makes the requester its player. Two players claiming the same character: the second receives 409. Players may still join with their own character.
- **DM sees and removes them**: the campaign details return the unclaimed premade characters to the DM only, apart from the party. The DM may delete an unclaimed premade character through the existing delete endpoint; once claimed, it belongs to the player like any other character.
- **Deleting a campaign deletes its unclaimed premade characters**, since they would be left with no player and no campaign. Claimed ones are kept, as today.
- The domain and repository accept a character without a player.

Out of scope: editing a premade character's name or max HP (no character has that today), and the DM handing a premade character to a specific user.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `premade-characters`: Who creates premade characters, where they appear, and who may delete them.

### Modified Capabilities
- `campaign-invites`: Invite details list the premade characters and accepting may claim one.
- `campaign-details`: The DM receives the unclaimed premade characters; the party and the player count only contain characters with a player.
- `campaign-deletion`: Unclaimed premade characters are deleted with the campaign.

## Impact

- **API**: new `POST /campaign/{campaignID}/character`; additive `premade_characters` in the invite details and campaign details; `DELETE /character/{characterID}` also allowed for the campaign DM on an unclaimed premade character; accept may return 409.
- **Code**: character domain (`PlayerID` optional, premade policy), pg rows and mapper (nullable `player_id`), new create use case, invite detail and accept use cases, campaign details and deletion, Swagger annotations (regenerated by CI).
- **Frontend**: the DM creates premade characters on the campaign page; the invite page offers them next to the player's own characters.
