# Proposal

## Why

Once a character joins a campaign it stays there until the character or the campaign is deleted. At a real table players leave, swap characters or are removed by the DM, and today the only way out is deleting the sheet.

## What Changes

- **A character can leave its campaign** through `DELETE /core/api/v1/campaign/{campaignID}/character/{characterID}`. The character's player may remove their own character (leaving), and the campaign DM may remove any player's character (kicking). The character stays with its player, unlinked from any campaign, like when a campaign is deleted.
- Not in ARCHIVED campaigns: the party of a finished campaign is its history.
- Unclaimed premade characters are not removed this way: the DM deletes them through the existing endpoint.

Out of scope: notifying the removed player (no notifications exist), a reason for the removal, and moving a character straight to another campaign (leave, then accept the other invite).

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `campaign-membership`: Who can remove a character from a campaign, and what happens to it.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

- **API**: new `DELETE /campaign/{campaignID}/character/{characterID}` (204).
- **Code**: character domain policy, a use case in the character module that loads the campaign through a port, a repository update unlinking the character, handler and Swagger annotation (regenerated by CI).
- **Frontend**: "Sair da campanha" on the player's sheet or campaign page, and "Remover" on each party card for the DM.
