# Proposal

## Why

The DM cannot join their own campaign as a player, but the invite details do not say who is reading them. Clients have no way to tell the DM apart from a player, so the frontend shows the DM the character picker and the DM only finds out after submitting, through a 403. The DM still has reasons to open the invite link: checking that it works and seeing what players will see.

## What Changes

- `GET /core/api/v1/invite/{inviteHash}` returns `is_dm`, true when the requester is the DM of the invite's campaign.
- Adds the invite details rules to the spec: any authenticated user holding the hash can read them, and an unknown invite returns 404 (current behavior, not documented yet).

Out of scope: changing who can read or accept an invite. The DM can still read their own invite, and accepting it keeps returning 403.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities
- `campaign-invites`: Adds the invite details requirement, including the requester's role.

## Impact

- **API**: Additive. `InviteDetailsResponse` gets the `is_dm` boolean; existing clients are unaffected.
- **Code**: invite details read model and mapper, get invite detail use case, HTTP response and mapper, Swagger annotation (regenerated by CI).
- **Frontend**: once released, the invite page can show the DM a "you are the DM of this campaign" notice with a link to the campaign instead of the character picker.
